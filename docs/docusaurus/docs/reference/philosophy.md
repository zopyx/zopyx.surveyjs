Privacy first, control always. `zopyx.surveyjs` is designed for
organizations that want powerful forms without sacrificing data
ownership.

## Principles

Privacy First  
Forms and submissions stay inside your Plone site. Data is stored in
your database and only leaves the system when you explicitly enable
integrations such as email or POST actions.

All on Your Server  
The module does not require any external SaaS service to function.
Optional integrations (AI, external validation) are opt-in and
configured by the administrator.

Transparent and Auditable  
Actions are explicit and predictable. Validation, storage, and exports
are performed in clear code paths with structured logging.

Practical Security  
Payload limits and validation controls are included to reduce abuse and
protect resources.
