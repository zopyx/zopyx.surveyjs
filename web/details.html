Overview

Privacy-first form infrastructure, built on SurveyJS and Plone.

Privacy Forms Studio combines modern survey UX with enterprise-grade governance. It keeps form logic flexible while keeping data processing firmly inside your infrastructure.

Highlights

  • SurveyJS-powered builder, viewer, and results center.
  • Secure data collection with configurable retention and routing.
  • Plone workflows for governance, publishing, and permissions.
  • Optional AI assistance that never forces data outside.

Features

Every stage of the form lifecycle, covered.

From authoring to distribution, Privacy Forms Studio keeps data traceable, exportable, and secure.

Storage

Store submissions within your infrastructure or route them immediately based on your retention policy.

Transformation

Convert data into TXT, HTML, PDF, Markdown, JSON, Excel, CSV, DOCX, or XML with configurable templates.

Distribution

Deliver submissions by email or push them to REST endpoints with per-form configuration.

AI support

Optional assistance for drafting and refining forms. Use your own provider or local LLMs.

Form versioning

Keep and restore previous versions with auditability built into the workflow.

Data portability

Definitions and submissions stay in structured JSON, ready to export or migrate.

Integrations

Consume data from REST endpoints and push results into existing backend systems.

Custom processing

Extend processing and distribution with Python workflows and subscriber hooks.

Power of Plone

Delegate permissions, schedule publication, and enforce governance with proven CMS tooling.

Why SurveyJS

SurveyJS-class UX, without the SaaS lock-in.

Privacy Forms Studio delivers the best of SurveyJS while keeping data governance in your hands.

Key capabilities

  • JSON-based, fully configurable forms and surveys.
  • Conditional logic, validation, and calculated fields.
  • Rich question types and repeatable sections.
  • Responsive, accessible UI for desktop and mobile.
  • Multi-language support and localization.
  • Visual builder plus code-level control.

Privacy-first architecture

  • No mandatory SaaS backend; all forms run in your infrastructure.
  • Full data sovereignty with zero hidden telemetry.
  • Ideal for GDPR/DSGVO-critical use cases.
  • Clear separation between logic and storage for auditability.
  • Extensible without vendor lock-in.

The result is modern UX with enterprise governance, even for privacy-sensitive and regulated environments.

Privacy

Privacy-first architecture, not an add-on.

Every deployment keeps data under your control, with clear configuration over what is stored, processed, or transmitted.

Full control over data

Submissions stay inside your infrastructure by default, with configurable retention and deletion.

No hidden data flows

External integrations are explicit and opt-in. Nothing is sent without your configuration.

Optional AI assistance

Use external providers or your own local LLMs for drafting and refinement.

Configurable privacy levels

Control how data is processed, stored, retained, or removed at any time.
