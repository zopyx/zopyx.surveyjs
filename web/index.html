About SurveyJS Licensing:

SurveyJS Creator requires a separate commercial license per developer if you want to use the visual form builder. SurveyJS Dashboard and PDF generator are optional add-ons. Privacy Forms Studio includes its own PDF generation. We help you plan the right setup for your needs.
