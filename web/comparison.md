## 3. Strategic Positioning of Privacy Forms Studio

### What Makes Privacy Forms Studio Different

1. **Privacy by default, not by configuration**  
   No SaaS competitor can claim “no external processing” as a default state.

2. **Data sovereignty without functional compromise**  
   Comparable logic and UX to leading SaaS tools—without handing data to a vendor.

3. **Governance + forms in one stack**  
   Tight integration with **Plone** enables permissions, workflows, audits, and content lifecycle management that form-only tools lack.

4. **Optional, controlled AI integration**  
   AI assistance can be enabled **only** with customer-chosen LLMs—on-premise or privacy-focused.

5. **Designed for regulated environments**  
   Public sector, healthcare, research, and enterprises where SaaS form builders are often legally or politically unacceptable.
