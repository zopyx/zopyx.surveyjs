Architecture

SurveyJS + Plone: separation of concerns done right

  • JSON-based schemas integrate cleanly with Plone content types and APIs.
  • Rich UX from SurveyJS; governance, permissions, and workflows from Plone.
  • Extensible without vendor lock-in—custom logic and validation remain yours.
  • Ready for modern frontends: REST, GraphQL, headless setups supported.

Deployment flavors

  • Plone add-on: embed inside existing Plone sites.
  • Standalone PFS server with full Plone capabilities.
  • Containerized (Docker) on-premise or managed SaaS.
  • Embeddable forms for your own web apps (planned).
