Key takeaway Privacy first

Data sovereignty

Keep survey data inside your own infrastructure while still delivering the same modern form experiences users expect from leading SaaS platforms. You stay in control of hosting, storage, backups, and access policies without sacrificing polish or usability.

Governance included

Approval workflows, audit trails, and role-based publishing are built in, not bolted on. This is critical for teams that must document decisions, enforce permissions, and comply with organizational or legal processes around data collection.

Optional AI

Use your own LLM or disable AI completely. No hidden data flows, and no obligation to send prompts or results outside your environment.

Market landscape

Two camps: SaaS convenience vs self-hosted control.

SaaS platforms optimize for speed and ease of onboarding, but they inherently route data through vendor infrastructure. Privacy-sovereign platforms protect data ownership, yet often give up governance and editorial depth. Privacy Forms Studio is positioned specifically to bridge that gap, pairing a modern SurveyJS-driven UX with the governance and data control expected in regulated organizations.

SaaS-first form builders

Vendor-hosted

Jotform

Drag-and-drop forms, large template ecosystem, optional EU data residency.

Privacy note: Data is always processed in vendor cloud infrastructure.

Typeform

Conversational UX with strong branding controls.

Privacy note: SaaS-only, EU hosting on higher tiers.

SurveyMonkey

Enterprise survey platform with compliance tooling.

Privacy note: EU hosting options, metadata may still be processed elsewhere.

Qualtrics

Advanced survey logic for enterprise research.

Privacy note: Vendor-operated SaaS with regional data centers.

Medallia XM

High-end experience management and feedback programs.

Privacy note: Contract-based data residency, not self-hosted.

Summary: SaaS competitors can provide GDPR features and contractual guarantees, but they cannot remove third-party data processing from the equation. That distinction becomes critical for public sector, healthcare, and research environments where data sovereignty is a hard requirement, not just a preference.

Self-hosted alternatives

Privacy sovereign

LimeSurvey CE

Fully self-hosted and open source.

Trade-off: Older UI and limited enterprise workflow integration.

Form.io

Developer-centric form plus API platform.

Trade-off: Less editorial and governance tooling by default.

Formbricks

Open-source, privacy-first positioning with self-host option.

Trade-off: Focuses on product feedback over enterprise workflows.

Nextcloud Forms

Simple, self-hosted surveys inside Nextcloud.

Trade-off: Limited form logic and UX depth.

Summary: Self-hosted tools protect privacy, but many lack enterprise-grade governance, publishing workflows, and audit capabilities. They are often great for developers, yet less suited for organizations that need structured approvals, role-based publishing, and long-term content lifecycle management.

Strategic positioning

Why Privacy Forms Studio stands apart.

The platform combines privacy-first architecture with the governance and workflow capabilities that regulated teams need. It is designed for environments where data must stay in-house and where published forms require clear ownership, review processes, and audit trails.

01

Privacy by default

No SaaS competitor can claim zero external processing as a default state. Privacy Forms Studio starts with data staying inside your infrastructure and only adds integrations when you explicitly configure them.

02

Data sovereignty without compromise

Match modern survey UX while retaining full control over where data lives. This balance removes the common trade-off between user experience and data sovereignty.

03

Governance plus forms

Plone integration adds permissions, workflows, audits, and lifecycle management. That means forms are not just created and shared, they are governed, reviewed, and tracked over time.

04

Optional, controlled AI

Enable AI with your chosen LLM or keep it fully offline. The decision stays with you, and no workflow requires external AI services.

05

Built for regulated teams

Public sector, healthcare, research, and enterprises that cannot use SaaS because of legal, political, or policy constraints. Privacy Forms Studio is built to meet those constraints from day one.

Outcome

Modern UX, sovereign control

SurveyJS-class experience with governance, auditability, and privacy built in. You get the modern form UX expected by users while meeting organizational mandates for data control and compliance.
